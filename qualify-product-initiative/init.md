# Default questions: Qualify product initiative

## Skill owner (innovation manager)
- How does an initiative get submitted for qualification?
- What triggers the qualification process?
- How are the four buckets weighted in the final decision?
- What does a qualified initiative look like — what happens next?
- What typically causes an initiative to be rejected or deferred?

## Development effort (architect)
- How do you estimate development effort at this company?
- What is the unit of estimation (days, story points, t-shirt)?
- What systems or tools do you consult when estimating?
- What automatically makes something high effort here?
- What dependencies or constraints are commonly missed?

## Strategic fit (innovation manager)
- Where are the company's strategic goals documented?
- How current are they and who owns them?
- What does strong strategic fit look like versus partial fit?
- Are there initiatives that would always be rejected on strategic 
  grounds regardless of other factors?

## Compliance and architecture (compliance officer + architect)
- Which compliance frameworks apply to new initiatives?
- Is compliance assessment a checklist or a judgment call?
- Who has sign-off authority on compliance?
- What are the most common architectural constraints?

## Market size and validated demand (innovation manager + sales)
- How does the company define validated demand?
- What counts as evidence of demand?
- Where does market data typically come from internally?
- How is judgment applied when data is incomplete?
