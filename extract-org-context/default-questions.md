# Default questions: extract-org-context

## Purpose
Extract company-wide organizational context into the shared 
context layer. This runs before any skill-specific calibration.
Covers four shared context files: org-structure, 
decision-making, data-sources, and terminology.

---

## 1. Org structure
**Output file:** shared/org-structure.md

### Questions
- What are the key departments in your organization?
- How do those departments relate to each other — 
  who reports to whom at the top level?
- Which departments are most involved in innovation, 
  product, or change initiatives?

**Sufficient if:** Names specific departments, describes 
reporting lines, identifies which teams are relevant to 
AI and process improvement
**Too high-level if:** "We have various teams" or generic 
org chart language without specifics
**Probe:** Can you name the departments and their heads? 
Who does the innovation or product team report to?

---

## 2. Decision making
**Output file:** shared/decision-making.md

### Questions
- How are significant decisions made in your organization — 
  who proposes, who reviews, who approves?
- Are decisions made centrally or within individual departments?
- What slows decisions down most often?

**Sufficient if:** Describes a real decision flow with named 
roles, identifies where bottlenecks typically occur
**Too high-level if:** "We have a management team that decides" 
without describing the actual flow
**Probe:** Walk me through a recent significant decision — 
who was involved and at what stage?

---

## 3. Data sources
**Output file:** shared/data-sources.md

### Questions
- What are the main systems your organization uses to store 
  and manage information? (eg. CRM, ERP, project tools, 
  document storage)
- Where do employees typically go to find information when 
  doing their work?
- Which systems are considered reliable versus outdated 
  or inconsistently used?

**Sufficient if:** Names specific tools with their purpose, 
identifies which are trusted sources versus noisy ones
**Too high-level if:** "We use various systems" or only 
naming tool categories without specifics
**Probe:** What tool would you open first if you needed to 
find the status of an ongoing project?

---

## 4. Terminology
**Output file:** shared/terminology.md

### Questions
- Are there company-specific terms, acronyms, or concepts 
  that an outsider would not immediately understand?
- How does your organization refer to key concepts like 
  initiatives, projects, teams, or stages of work?
- Are there terms that mean something different here than 
  they would at a typical company in your sector?

**Sufficient if:** Provides a list of specific terms with 
definitions or context, flags where standard terms have 
non-standard meanings
**Too high-level if:** "We have some internal jargon" 
without examples
**Probe:** If I joined your team tomorrow, what are the 
first three terms I would need to learn to follow 
conversations?
